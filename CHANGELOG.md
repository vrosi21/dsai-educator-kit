## 3: Your course can also be changed on the site (13 October 2026)

- The site now has an editor: on any page of your course press Edit. Every kind of content has a form with a preview, images can be added, and History shows what was changed and brings an earlier version back.
- The kit works as before. Both change the same course: after anything was changed on the site, download the course again before you work here.
- Your assistant is told to leave what was made on the site as it is: the layout of those files, and images named by a string of hex digits.
